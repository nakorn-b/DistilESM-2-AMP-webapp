# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Peer reviewers, researchers, and lab scientists. They are likely in a high-focus environment, validating scientific results or processing experimental data.

## Product Purpose
A prediction portal for Antimicrobial Peptides (AMPs). It provides a high-reliability interface for uploading CSV datasets for batch classification or testing single sequences, with the goal of providing clear, downloadable, and labeled results with confidence scores.

## Brand Personality
Academic, Trustworthy, Precise. The interface should feel like a reliable piece of scientific equipment—functional, transparent, and authoritative.

## Anti-references
- Notion-style minimalism (can feel too "casual" or "productivity-focused").
- Boring AI-generated "hint" patterns (generic chatbots or over-the-top "magical" sparks).
- Outdated, cluttered government or university portals.

## Design Principles
- **Scientific Authority**: Use clear typography and data-heavy layouts that prioritize legibility over decoration.
- **Functional Transparency**: Always show confidence scores and labels clearly; never hide logic behind "AI magic."
- **High-Density Utility**: Focus on efficient workflows for batch processing and CSV management.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA compliance.
- High contrast for data visualization and labels.
- Support for keyboard-driven navigation in batch workflows.
